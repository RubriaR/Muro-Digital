# El Muro Digital

## Description
*El Muro Digital* is a choice-driven electronic literature piece following two siblings on a fraught journey to the U.S. border during a family medical emergency. It explores the agonizing decisions shaped by systemic barriers, family separation, and the invisible borders of the migrant experience. 

👉 View the project: https://rubriar.github.io/Muro-Digital/ 

## Artistic Statement
*El Muro Digital* explores how bureaucratic systems and technological divides act as insurmountable barriers for separated families. The interactive narrative places the reader within the agonizing constraints of a cross-border medical emergency, exposing the invisible walls of language, resources, and distance that fracture relationships and deny the basic right to say goodbye.
